How to responsibly dispose of expired medication.

Improper disposal of antibiotics, antidepressants, and hormones contaminates regional water tables and encourages antimicrobial resistance. MedSafe enforces safe on-premise neutralization.

Method 1: Household Neutralization

1. Mix uncrushed tablets or liquid medication with an unpalatable substance (used coffee grounds, dirt, or cat litter).
2. Seal the mixture in a durable ziplock bag or airtight container.
3. Black out patient identification and prescription details on the original bottle.
4. Discard the sealed mixture with standard household waste.

Method 2: Authorized Pharmacy Take-Back Dropboxes

Most certified hospital pharmacies and retail pharmacies offer secure, anonymous drop-box kiosks for expired controlled medications and schedule IV compounds.
